My Journey

From refusing Silicon Valley to building the next generation of software architecture

I refused to follow the Silicon Valley playbook. When everyone chased the hype cycle and built models no one needed, I asked a different question: What if we could build software that lasts?

My philosophy is simple: efficiency first, hype never. I spent years auditing 3000+ products and realized the pattern—companies fail not because they lack AI, but because they build the wrong architecture with the wrong teams.

Now I'm on a mission. 30+ companies are collaborating with me on this journey. I'm hiring the brightest minds. And I'm traveling the world (next: Spain) to prove that post-AI software is built on resilience, not trends.

Jonathan Seagull refused to be average. So do I.
